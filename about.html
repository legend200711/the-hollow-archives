About the Archives

For the stories that wait until the lights go out.

OUR PURPOSE

Horror has no season.

The Hollow Archives is a year-round home for original horror fiction, eerie encounters, strange legends, and the stories that make familiar places feel unfamiliar.

We believe a good scary story can be told in a single paragraph or unfold across a hundred pages. What matters is the feeling it leaves behind after the final line.

A carefully kept collection

Stories submitted by visitors are reviewed before publication. The archive's keeper chooses what becomes public, and submissions are never automatically published.
