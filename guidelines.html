BEFORE YOU SEND IT INTO THE DARK

Submission Guidelines

A few rules keep the archive safe for everyone.

What we're looking for

  • Original horror fiction, eerie encounters, supernatural tales, urban legends, and unsettling mysteries.
  • Stories with a clear title and a complete text.
  • Pen names are welcome; using a pen name is optional.

Ownership and permission

Submit only work you created or have permission to share. You retain ownership of your original work. If a story is selected, publication permission and any applicable terms should be confirmed with the site owner before it goes live.

Review and publication

Every submission is reviewed. Sending a story does not guarantee publication. Stories are never automatically published, and the keeper may reject submissions or remove published stories.

Privacy and safety

Do not include private information about yourself or other people. If you provide contact information, it should only be used for submission-related follow-up. A privacy policy and contact method should be finalized before the website publicly accepts submissions.
